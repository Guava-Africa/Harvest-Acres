Who We Are

Harvest Acres is a proudly Zimbabwean beverage company — from purified mineral water and fruit juices to cordials and ready-to-drink Tamba blends. We craft and distribute refreshing drinks using natural juices, concentrates, and powders while empowering local partnerships and championing sustainable practices.

  • Health-focused products free from artificial colours, preservatives, and sweeteners
  • Locally sourced ingredients supporting Zimbabwean farmers and communities
  • Supplying supermarkets, hospitality, corporate clients, and homes nationwide
