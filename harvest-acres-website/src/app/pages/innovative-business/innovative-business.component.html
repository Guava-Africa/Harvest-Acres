Our Enterprise

Harvest Acres runs a varied beverage enterprise — from purified mineral water and fruit juices to cordials and ready-to-drink Tamba blends. We are actively expanding into sugarcane juice, flavoured and sparkling waters, iced teas, herbal infusions, and new ready-to-drink formats.

Backed by a robust retail and wholesale network, we reach customers through supermarkets, hospitality venues, corporate supply, and direct delivery — proudly serving Zimbabwe with naturally inspired beverages at scale.
