About Harvest Acres

Harvest Acres is a proudly Zimbabwean beverage company dedicated to producing high-quality, natural drinks while empowering local farmers and championing sustainability.

Our Vision

To be Zimbabwe’s leading provider of natural and innovative beverage solutions, delivering freshness, health, and sustainability across Africa.

Our Mission

To craft high-quality beverages that nourish communities while promoting sustainability, innovation, and economic empowerment through local sourcing.

Our Core Values

Quality First

We prioritize excellence in every product we deliver.

Sustainability

We are committed to environmentally responsible practices.

Innovation

We continuously evolve to meet modern consumer needs.

Community

We support and uplift local farmers and communities.
