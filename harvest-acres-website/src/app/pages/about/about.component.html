Company Overview

A Proudly Zimbabwean Beverage Enterprise

Harvest Acres runs a varied beverage enterprise — currently featuring products like fruit juices and purified mineral water, with offerings expanding to include sugarcane juice, flavoured and sparkling waters, iced teas, herbal infusions, cordials, and ready-to-drink juices.

With a robust retail and wholesale network, we supply supermarkets, hotels, restaurants, corporate offices, and consumers directly — crafting health-focused beverages free from artificial colours, preservatives, and sweeteners, with locally sourced ingredients supporting Zimbabwean farmers.

Vision Statement

Our Vision

To be Zimbabwe’s leading provider of accessible, health-conscious beverages — blending innovation, quality, and sustainability to refresh the nation.

Mission Statement

Our Mission

“To craft and distribute a diverse range of refreshing beverages — from purified water and fruit-based drinks using natural juices, concentrates, and powders — while promoting healthy lifestyles, empowering local partnerships, and championing sustainable practices.”
